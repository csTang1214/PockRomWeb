Built to be yours, private, and actually playable

Every design choice in PocketRomance comes back to three things: it has to run on your phone, it has to stay yours, and it has to feel alive.

⚡

Runs on low-end phones

Pick from four on-device AI models right in the app — a tiny, ultra-fast model built for budget hardware, up to a larger model for deeper conversations. You choose the tradeoff, so your companion is never too heavy for your phone.

🔒

Total privacy, fully offline

There's no chat server and no account. Once the app and a model are downloaded, every conversation, voice line, and memory is generated and stored locally — nothing is uploaded anywhere.

🎨

Deep character customization

Unlimited offline chat

Frequently asked questions

Is PocketRomance really free?

Yes. PocketRomance is free to download and use, with no subscription and no account required. Download the app and one AI model (a one-time download), then chat as much as you like.

Does it work without internet or wifi?

Yes. After the one-time app and model download, chatting, voice, and memory all run entirely on your phone. Talk to your companion on a plane, underground, or anywhere with zero signal.

Will PocketRomance run on my phone if it's not a flagship?

Yes. Pick from four on-device AI model sizes right in the app — from a tiny model tuned for instant replies on budget phones, to a larger model for richer conversation — so you can match performance to your device.

Is my data private?

Yes. Your characters, conversations, and memories are stored only as local files on your own phone. There's no cloud account and no chat server — your conversations are never uploaded anywhere.

Can I customize how she looks and acts?

Yes. Mix and match face shape, hairstyle and color, eyes and eyebrows, and 15+ outfits, then choose from 10+ personality traits, a voice, and whether the relationship is romantic or platonic — for up to 6 characters at once.

Is PocketRomance appropriate for kids?

No. PocketRomance is intended for adults 18 and older. Every character is clearly AI-generated, not a real person, and the app includes an age gate and built-in content-safety checks.

What devices does PocketRomance support?

PocketRomance is currently available for Android, on Google Play.
